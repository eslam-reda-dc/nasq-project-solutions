What a Senior Document Controller Should Report to Management

Management needs visibility of project information, review bottlenecks and decision risks—not a long list of unexplained document numbers.

Overdue actions

Document-flow risks

Flag missing references, uncontrolled revisions, incomplete submissions and weak handover readiness.
