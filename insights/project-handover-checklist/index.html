DOCUMENT CONTROL FROM THE FIELD

A Practical Project Document Handover Checklist

A handover should be prepared progressively throughout the project, not assembled from scattered folders during the final week.

Remote deliveryEgypt & Saudi ArabiaConfidential handling
Discuss on WhatsAppRequest a scope review

Confirm the required deliverables

Use the contract, project procedures and approved handover requirements to identify what the client expects.

Reconcile final approvals

Check approved drawings, material records, inspections and other required submissions against the final register.

Organize as-built information

Verify the latest as-built issue, discipline coverage and the relationship between drawings and project records.

Identify gaps early

Issue a missing-items list with responsibility, current status and an agreed follow-up route.

Preserve a searchable archive

Use a predictable folder structure, meaningful filenames and controlled indexes so the recipient can find records later.
