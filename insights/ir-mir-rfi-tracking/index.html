DOCUMENT CONTROL FROM THE FIELD

IR, MIR and RFI: What Project Management Should Track

A useful document dashboard does more than count submissions. It highlights the decision status, elapsed time, responsible party and next action.

Remote deliveryEgypt & Saudi ArabiaConfidential handling

Track the correct document type

Inspection requests, material inspections and requests for information serve different operational decisions and should not be mixed in one unexplained total.

Measure age and overdue status

Record the submission date, contractual or agreed response window, current status and the date of the latest action.

Connect comments to resubmission

Rejected or revise-and-resubmit items should clearly show who owns the corrective action and which revision is expected next.

Report for decisions, not decoration

Management should be able to identify approvals needed, operational blockers and items requiring consultant or contractor follow-up.
